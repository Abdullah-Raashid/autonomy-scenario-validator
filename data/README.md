# Sample data

The original `sample_*.csv` scenario is preserved. It intentionally produces
collisions and lane departures under the corrected 4.5 m long, 2 m wide ego
footprint. `dynamic_*.csv` adds a crossing obstacle and a slower lead vehicle.

`synthetic_road.png` is generated by `asv::vision::SyntheticRoad()` with seed 42,
960x540 pixels. Its straight lane markings have known bottom x coordinates
0.20*(width-1) and 0.80*(width-1). It contains mild deterministic noise and
horizontal distractors. No external road imagery is included. Regenerate with:

```sh
./build-vision/lane_detection_demo --synthetic --output outputs/lanes
```

Coordinates, CSV schemas and obstacle epoch semantics are specified in
[algorithms](../docs/ALGORITHMS.md).
